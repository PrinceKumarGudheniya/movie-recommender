    How It Works

    1
    Feature Soup

    For each movie we concatenate genres, cast, director and overview into one text string. Genres & director are repeated to boost their weight.

    2
    TF-IDF Vectorisation

    Term Frequency–Inverse Document Frequency converts every soup string into a high-dimensional sparse vector, emphasising rare but meaningful words.

    3
    Cosine Similarity

    The angle between two vectors is measured: score = (A · B) / (‖A‖ × ‖B‖). A score of 1 means identical features; 0 means no overlap at all.

    4
    Ranked Results

    Movies are sorted by descending similarity score and the top-8 are returned as personalised recommendations.
